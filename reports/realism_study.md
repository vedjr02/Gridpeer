## Caveats

- **Synthetic data.** Every number comes from `data.synthetic`, not Irish smart-meter data. Real CER data needs an ISSDA access request.
- **No seasons in the generator.** The season table scales solar uniformly; a real winter also changes demand (heating, longer evenings).
- **Policies not re-optimised** for scale, season or network charges — the tables show how today's trained policies generalise, not the best achievable.
- **Flat tariffs.** One import and one export price for everyone, all day. Time-of-use tariffs would change the battery results most.
